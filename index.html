Said before buying — these are conversion problems

Hidden fees and surprise charges
Hidden fees can significantly impact your marketing budget and can lead to unexpected financial burdens.
— fastercapital.com
Prospects are deterred by unexpected costs revealed late, undermining trust and increasing cart abandonment.
Misleading advertised prices
The user is enticed with a low advertised price. After investing time and effort, they discover unexpected fees and charges when they reach the checkout.
— deceptive.design
Customers feel deceived when the final price differs from the advertised one, leading to distrust and negative reviews.
Confusing pricing structures
Other ways some businesses tend to overcomplicate pricing include hidden fees, inconsistent pricing across channels, frequent price changes, convoluted bundling, and confusing loyalty programs.
— netsuite.com
Complex pricing with hidden fees or inconsistent rates confuses customers and complicates their decision-making.
Unclear refund policies
If your refund policy is unclear, it can harm customer experience and make your roadmap for processing requests difficult.
— squarespace.com
Ambiguous return or refund terms create anxiety before purchase and can deter potential buyers.
Unexpected fees at checkout
Junk fees are unexpected, hidden, or mandatory charges added to the advertised price of a product or service, often revealed late in the checkout process or after the purchase.
— alliantcreditunion.org
Surprise fees at the final step of purchase lead to frustration and abandoned carts, as customers feel trapped.
Lack of upfront transparency
New rules now force upfront transparency for live events and short term lodging, but consumers still benefit from vigilance elsewhere: compare final prices, read disclosures, ask questions, and report deceptive practices.
— alliantcreditunion.org
Customers expect total price transparency from the start; hidden costs damage trust and increase perceived risk.
